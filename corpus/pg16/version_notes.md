## [PG16-SCOPE] Corpus scope
This index covers PostgreSQL major version 16 only. Guidance for other major versions (12, 13, 14, 15, 17, 18) is not in this corpus; column names and settings differ between majors, for example inactive_since in pg_replication_slots exists only in later versions. Requests for another major version must be escalated to a human.
